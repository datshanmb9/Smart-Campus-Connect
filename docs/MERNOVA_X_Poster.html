01

Abstract

Modern higher educational institutions manage extensive physical infrastructure spanning blocks, lecture halls, and laboratories. Despite high tech investments, communication remains fragmented across paper registers, physical notice boards, and unorganized messaging groups.

Smart Campus Connect is an enterprise full-stack platform unifying classroom issue reporting, conflict-free room reservations, event ticketing, and lost-and-found tracking. Powered by a single Node.js/Express REST API, a cloud MongoDB Atlas cluster, a React 18 admin dashboard, and a Flutter Android app with smart classroom QR scanning, it delivers sub-second response times, zero-latency audit histories, and seamless cross-platform synchronization.
02

Problem Statement

Higher education campuses face three major operational breakdowns daily:
  • Fault Reporting Lag: Equipment breakdowns (projectors, lab PCs, Wi-Fi) take days to report and fix due to vague descriptions ("projector in 2nd floor").
  • Venue Clashing: Manual room booking registers cause embarrassing double-bookings during workshops and guest lectures.
  • Information Silos: Event circulars on physical boards go unnoticed, while lost belongings posted in chat groups breach student contact privacy.
03

Objectives

  • Instant 30s Fault Reporting: Enable classroom QR code scanning (e.g., PU-ENGG-LH204) with photo proof to instantly geotag fault tickets.
  • 100% Conflict-Free Reservations: Implement pre-commit algorithmic validation strictly preventing double-booking of shared campus spaces.
  • Audit Transparency & SLA Tracking: Maintain complete immutable audit logs from ticket creation to technician resolution.
  • Single Cloud Backend: Synchronize web administrators and mobile students via unified REST endpoints and MongoDB Atlas.
04

Proposed Solution

Smart Campus Connect re-engineers university administration into a mobile-first, event-driven ecosystem:
  • Smart Lectern QR Tags: Students scan classroom QR tags to auto-populate venue details, attach live camera photos, and submit repair tickets in under 30 seconds.
  • Administrative Command Portal: Real-time React dashboard allows estate managers to assign maintenance technicians, moderate lost & found claims, and approve bookings.
  • Audited Ticket Lifecycle: Sequential ticketing (CMP-2026-XXXX) with status transitions (Open → In Progress → Resolved).
  • Privacy-Shielded Claims: Contact information is protected until ownership is substantiated.
